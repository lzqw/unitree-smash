The optional `--late-arm-rate` retains all24 parameters and their original amplitude bounds, but applies the six rate corrections over the final8 ticks (80ms), not the whole330ms prefix. The cubic g(s)=s³−s² is zero before onset, starts with zero position/rate and returns to zero endpoint position. Position-endpoint basis remains unchanged. The empirical reserve inherited from the previous basis is only an initial heuristic, not a calibrated new-basis error bound. All derivatives and final proposals are recomputed.

## Matched-context negative evidence

To avoid attributing changes in baseline to the basis, a separate deterministic audit repeated only the original six rate derivative probes at exactly the same119.511mm baseline, initial state, reference history, targets and amplitudes. The baseline q/v is reproduced exactly; all six old-basis derivatives fully pass. No optimizer proposal or native candidate is generated by this audit.

Using the intersection of both parameter bounds, the optimistic local projection along the desired velocity-error reduction direction is:

- Original330ms basis: 0.100563021m/s.
- Late80ms basis: 0.050923720m/s.

These are local linear projections ignoring coupled path/terminal constraints, not certified speed bounds or globally attainable velocities. Still, at this matched state they contradict the intended speed-authority benefit. The late basis is retained only as an optional documented experiment, not promoted to default. A larger reference endpoint derivative does not guarantee larger actual racket velocity through the implicit PD/fullbody response.
