# Scaled push-off QP replay

The causal push-off support QP was replayed with the requested COM acceleration
scaled to 10 percent. The path passed all 29 intervals and adopted 11 QP
commands. Terminal errors remained poor at position 591.874 mm, normal 0.267345
and racket velocity 4.450289 m/s. The scaled request was rejected as a strike
prefix and was never installed.

This confirms that the full QP request is not simply too large. The current
support-QP response is mismatched to the contact target. Native evaluation,
training and SuperMimic operation were not run.
