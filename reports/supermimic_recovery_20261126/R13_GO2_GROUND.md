# Native faults and clearance of the Go2 collision surfaces

On complete R13 PPO01 trajectories, the minimum clearance of the authored base, hip, thigh and calf collision surfaces was at most 1 mm at the first active native body fault in all 127 training-side cases and all 19 held cases. Across the entire eligible window it also fell below that tolerance in 3 of 95 training-side and 1 of 12 held cases without native body faults. The nearest surface was the left rear thigh in 106/127 and 17/19 fault cases. These observations use native force flags and geometry computed from the control-step endpoints. They do not identify the link that delivered the first substep force. On disjoint R12 trajectories the same surfaces covered 153/153 training and 20/20 held fault onsets, with zero false flags in 82 eligible nonfall cases. Sixteen ticks before late R13 training-side body faults, median clearance of the left rear thigh was 41 mm. At the same lead before first clean relaxed recovery it was 94 mm, with a tenth percentile of 75 mm. On 24 held R13 cases eligible throughout the recorded 16-step action intervention, three independently fitted WM members each predicted the correct sign on all 17 left-rear-thigh clearance changes greater than 1 mm. The aggregate direction cosines were 0.885, 0.931 and 0.910. Only two held cases had minimum clearance changes over 1 mm. The sign test is about this specific action intervention with observed actuator commands. [Paired response](r13_go2_ground_response.json)

An optional training objective now evaluates analytic lower support distances of 21 Go2 collision primitives using differentiable FK. Modeled survival falls on first entry into the 1–10 mm ground margin. A bounded landed reward for the left rear thigh reaches its maximum of 0.06 per tick at 75 mm. The controller, collision rules, native failure labels and official scoring are unchanged. The authored surfaces matched an independent support-witness implementation on random rotations and gave finite pose derivatives. The loss has also been backpropagated through PPO01 and all three WMs on matched native starts. [Parameter gradient contributions](r13_go2_ground_gradients.json)

The earlier weak-tail candidates are already in native evaluation. The geometry objective can be trained from PPO01 after evaluation if that block fails its official gate. No native policy improvement is claimed for this objective yet.
