# Native short returns on paired Recovery trajectories

The incumbent and absorbing-risk challenger ran full PhysX trajectories on R13. For the 121 cases with exact measured release prefixes and observations, the estimate uses actual rewards over the first H steps after the gate opened and discounts the frozen PPO01 Critic at the actual resulting observation. The comparison uses the difference in complete native discounted Recovery returns. The released branches continue under different policies. This experiment cannot isolate Critic error from the effect of later policy changes.

On the 13 held cases, 6 showed a native difference exceeding one return unit. At H16 the estimated change had RMS 0.029 versus native RMS 5.904, and matched the sign in 3 of the 6 cases. At H48 the estimate had RMS 1.468 and matched 4 of 6. Among 108 training-side pairs H16 sign agreement was 30 of 60 above the same threshold. These are differences between two runs of one candidate direction. They do not establish a universal gradient sign.

A training-side native intervention can address the continuation-policy ambiguity. It uses a common incumbent prefix, switches only the Recovery head for 16 control ticks, and restores the incumbent in both Approach streams. The comparisons include only active lanes whose recorded physical states, observations, requests and actuator targets match through the specified prefix. At tick 336 the original R13 training archive contains 221 active landed training lanes, of which 57 reach body contact or a low-root termination within 16 ticks. Tick selection used training lanes only. Training of the existing candidate and its fixed128 decision proceed separately.
