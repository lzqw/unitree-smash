# Preserving incumbent actions outside predicted pre-fall states

The previous weak-tail candidate created eleven new body contacts and prevented five on the 108 qualified official cases. The new optional step distills a trained BPTT candidate's corrections into the original six Recovery tensors. A training-only classifier uses observable joint, base and foot history and labels from R13 training cases. It scores risk of first body contact within 48 ticks. Twelve consecutive high scores identify a state for retaining the full BPTT correction. Other states target the incumbent action. The threshold uses the ninetieth percentile of peak sustained scores in 41 clean training cases. Deployment uses the existing policy network.

In the separate R12 behavior archive, a timely warning occurred 16–48 ticks before first body contact in 85/133 early and 30/40 late falls. Four of 42 clean relaxed cases also received a warning. This is a retrospective test of state discrimination. [Case counts](r13_localized_warning.json)

For the moderate candidate, on the same 42 clean R12 cases the RMS change of the final clipped normalized request relative to PPO01 fell from 0.00866 to 0.00276. In warned R12 states from 165 cases the request retained a projected gain of 0.962 and a direction cosine of 0.986 relative to the original BPTT correction. R12 contributed no examples to fitting this warning model or distillation. R13 and R12 are development archives. [Native observation replay](r13_localized_requests.json)

Both fitted policies changed exactly the six Recovery tensors. The conservative and moderate request RMS on the original action budget panel were 0.00440 and 0.00877. Native baseline replay error was below 0.000001. If the source candidate fails the official gate, these two localized candidates can receive their own paired PhysX evaluation using the already completed 20 BPTT updates.
