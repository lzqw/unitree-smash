# Native Recovery feedback search

The recovery branch now schedules native feedback search from PPO01. The pending airborne replay and first-support pulse jobs were cancelled before acquiring the GPU. The previous official fixed128 reference has 71 body contacts, 10 strict and 25 relaxed recoveries among 108 qualified shots, with 96 ball completions and zero net faults.

The resident search uses 128 PhysX environments, eight parameter vectors on the same 16 launches per generation, and 16 generations covering 256 training launches. The nominal parameter vector is evaluated every generation. The 24 feedback gains act on post-release joint requests before actuator clipping. Their inputs cover landed posture bias, left roll and roll rate, low body height, pitch and airborne rear-leg preparation. The correction ramps in after 50 ms. Both historical Approach heads participate. Each generation reports native results and how often RL calf requests move off their lower clip.

Teacher selection requires an observed nominal fall, a strict clean recovery on the candidate, preserved ball-side score, a matched intervention tick and observations within 1e-4 at that tick. Observations and raw corrections come from the winning branch. Nominal strict successes provide retention examples. On completion the runner fits the six existing Recovery tensors and evaluates the resulting checkpoint on full fixed128 when both pools are available. The search fitness and same-scene teacher comparisons are exploratory; policy performance is assessed separately.

CPU tests covered feedback indexing and phase gates, generation layout, terminal scoring flow and teacher selection using a simulated test harness. A second CPU smoke test used the actual PPO01 checkpoint and archived observations with synthetic labels to verify that distillation changes exactly the six Recovery tensors. At registration the native search was waiting for GPU availability. No search or fixed128 scores are reported here.
